I don't really know about their main problem. And a series C fintech will rarely make a decision of whether to use or not an enterprise software out of a 5 minute loom. So really my objective here is to make the VP curious enough to give us a short call to start with.

That's why I mentioned Bilt's case and the demo I had done of a common internal tool for the other series C fintech. Like what are their most important initiatives that could drive the whole org to be more efficient. To look at what could be automated and estimate the return (and WTP).
